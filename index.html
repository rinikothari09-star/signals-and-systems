Selected case studies

What this looks like in practice.

A few examples of the organizational bottlenecks, alignment problems, and execution gaps I've helped teams work through.

01

Reducing roadmap churn by 40% through outcome-based planning

The challenge

A fragmented operating model. Teams tracked different metrics, with no clear line between work and outcomes — and the roadmap changed every quarter.

The approach

Built an OKR-driven operating system that connected all work to business outcomes, with clear ownership and accountability.

The outcome

Teams moved faster with confidence. Roadmap stability improved sharply, and a major modernization accelerated by six months.

−40%scope churn

02

Avoiding a 4-month delay and unblocking 20+ customer go-lives

The challenge

Operations wouldn't support a critical feature without a full UI — a stance that would slip delivery four months and block 20+ customer implementations.

The approach

Reframed the conflict as phased delivery and designed a low-risk path that addressed Operations' concerns while protecting the commercial timeline.

The outcome

The feature shipped on schedule. Customers launched without delay, with zero production incidents — and Operations gained confidence in the approach.

4modelay avoided
20+customers unblocked
0incidents
03

Saving $1.5M while accelerating product innovation

The challenge

Sales needed innovation to win deals. Operations needed a legacy end-of-life to cut costs. Both were valid, both urgent, and the executive team was deadlocked.

The approach

Built a dual-track operating model — optimizing the legacy platform to free up resources and create dedicated capacity for innovation.

The outcome

Both priorities moved forward. The cost savings funded innovation, and the executive team aligned around a roadmap that worked for everyone.
