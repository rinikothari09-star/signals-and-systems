Who this is for

If any of this sounds familiar.

Your leaders can't agree on the problem

Product blames the go-to-market plan. Sales blames the roadmap. Operations blames both. Everyone's partly right. Nobody moves.

Decisions get slower as you scale

More people in the room. More perspectives. More "let's take this offline." Calls that should take a week take a quarter.

Your best people are getting frustrated

Not because of pay. Because the organization feels stuck. Talented people can't move anything forward, so they quietly check out.

You're adding headcount to fix it

You hired into the problem and velocity didn't change. Maybe it got worse. The bottleneck isn't capacity. It's clarity.

The roadmap looks good. Execution doesn't.

You planned well. Teams are busy. But outcomes aren't landing. Things ship; adoption and revenue don't follow.

Everyone's working harder, accomplishing less

More meetings, more threads, more motion. It feels productive. But nothing decisive gets shipped, and the org is thrashing.

Your business isn't broken. It has outgrown the systems that got it here.
